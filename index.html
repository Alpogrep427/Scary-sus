LAB BREACH

Gorilla-style VR with extended arm reach: swing and push off floors, benches, crates and walls, or hold grip on a surface to latch on and climb hand over hand. As the monster, grip near a body part grabs it instead. Join a room code; when 2 players vote, one becomes the monster. Arms rip off without killing; lose your head or get torn in half and you are out. The first player to enter a code hosts the room; friends who enter the same code join them directly (peer to peer, no game server). If the host leaves, the room ends. Turn sound on.

Desktop: click to look, WASD + Shift move, Space jump, Q/E grip left/right hand, hold R to pull hands back. Hover a part until the hand glows, grip, then pull away.
